Connect your wallet

Connect the wallet you are moving assets out of. Balances are read from 32 networks. Nothing is sent until you approve it in your wallet.

Fewer approvals with a different wallet

Your wallet approves every transfer separately. With your current selection that is 0 approvals. A wallet that supports batched transfers (EIP-5792) can approve a whole chain in one tap.

Support is still rolling out, so check the current list at eip5792.xyz. Smart-account wallets, and wallets that offer a smart-account upgrade for regular accounts (MetaMask does), are the usual candidates.

If you switch, use the wallet's official app or extension on your own device, and never type a recovery phrase into any website, including this one. Migrating with more approvals is always an option.
